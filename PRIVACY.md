# Privacy Policy

The amr-elnowahy-skills plugin contains only instruction files (skills).

- It does not collect, store or share any personal data.
- It includes no MCP servers, scripts or connections to outside services.
- The author receives no data from your use of the plugin.

Claude itself processes your conversations and code under Anthropic's own privacy policy.

Questions: open an issue at https://github.com/AmrKhaledNowahy/claude-skills/issues
